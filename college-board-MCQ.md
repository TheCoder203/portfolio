## Score
On the MCQ, I got a 66/66. However, I did search some of the answers where I was truly confused. This was about 10-15 of the questions.

![score](/images/MCQ.png)

A lot of the questions were common sense. These were pretty simple to answer. I did not struggle with these ones.

Some of the questions that were a bit more challenging were the coding questions, but after analyzing the code, it was pretty easy to spot the correct answer. 

Where things got hard and required looking up were the encryption problems. I wasn't sure how to do them, and would have definietly gotten it wrong if I hadn't search it up.

Overall, I think I can learn a lot from thsi MCQ. Not just what I did wrong, but also techniques on how to get questions right with less time. This will help me do better on the actual AP exam.
